About me

My story and background.

My story

I trained as a civil engineer, then spent two years as a Project Coordinator in CBRE South Asia's project management consultancy and design-and-build teams. On projects from 100,000 to 1 million sq. ft. for clients such as Avalara, Capgemini and WeWork, my job was business analysis in practice: gathering and validating requirements, mapping delivery processes to find gaps, chairing progress meetings and explaining cost and schedule trade-offs so stakeholders could decide.

That work produced measurable results: a 15% saving against the client's procurement budget on the Avalara project, a 10% saving against an additional change-order budget, and a 100% compliance record across my projects. I built Power BI and Excel dashboards to bring cost, quality, delivery and safety data together for management.

I've since completed an MSc in Business Analytics (Operational Research & Risk Analysis) at Alliance Manchester Business School (results pending), adding forecasting, optimisation and machine learning to that foundation, so I can help organisations turn complex technical and data problems into decisions people can act on.
